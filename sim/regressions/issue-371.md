The scripted scenario `interruptedConflictMove` (`sim/scenarios.ts`) kills the app while a create refused with `ALREADY_EXISTS` moves to its conflict copy: just after the journal records the copy's operation, before the refused one is removed (#371; the other device then frees the path, and the refused operation used to commit there while the file stayed at the copy, so every later push was refused), or just before it (#372; the user then deletes or moves the copy, and the refused operation used to stay pending forever).
