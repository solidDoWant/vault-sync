`seed-5-restores-lineage-faithful-fs.json` (#861) is seed 5 of the `restores` suite, minimized on master after #863 (139 steps, one backup and two restores). Its failure was in the harness, not the engine: `identity: e3 committed e3.x7 at notes/f (conflict …).md … from inode v1, which is c1.d1's file`. The harness's server seq (`RunContext.serverSeq`) was a running maximum that a restore never lowered, so engine writes made after the restore were stamped with the old server's head, after the restored server's later commits. The lineage replay then saw the conflict copy committed before the copy's file existed. Since the fix the restore resets the clock (`HarnessSeq.restoredTo`) and clamps earlier stamps (`clampToRestore`), and the case passes.
